Support

Answers to the questions that come up most, and how to get in touch.

The map is blank

Wanderer fetches the map as you look at it, so a part you haven't seen before needs a signal. Anything you've already looked at is kept on the phone. Before going somewhere remote, download the area: More → Download area for offline.

Routes are straight lines

Check that the snap button in the plan bar is on (blue). If it is, the planner hasn't got the footpaths for that area: it downloads them as you plan, but needs a signal to do it. Download the area with footpaths switched on before you go, and planning will work offline. A leg is also straight if there is no path near a point, or no path joins two points that the chosen kind of trip may use, such as a road bike on a public footpath. A short message in the planner says which.

Recording stopped

Wanderer needs to use your location while it's recording, including when the phone is locked. If iPhone asks, choose Allow While Using App; a blue indicator then shows in the status bar while recording. Low Power Mode doesn't stop recording, but turning off Location Services in Settings will. If Wanderer was closed, open it again: the recording is kept and can be resumed.

Ascent shows 0

Wanderer measures height with the barometer, which needs permission for Motion & Fitness. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → Wanderer. Ascent also ignores changes of under a metre or two, so a flat walk may honestly show very little.

Nothing on the lock screen

Check that Show on lock screen while recording is on in Wanderer's settings, and that Live Activities are allowed for Wanderer in the iPhone Settings app.

Finding GPX files

Recorded tracks and saved routes are written as GPX files you can open in the Files app, under iCloud Drive → Wanderer (or On My iPhone → Wanderer if iCloud Drive is off), in the Tracks and Routes folders. You can also send one from the share button in the saved routes list or a route's details.

Routes not on my other device

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive switched on for Wanderer: in the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then iCloud Drive. Wanderer's own Settings, under Saved routes and tracks, says whether it is using iCloud Drive. New routes usually appear on the other device within a minute while it has a connection.

Walks not in Fitness

Check that Save to Apple Health & Fitness is on in Wanderer's settings, and that Wanderer may write workouts in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → Wanderer). Tracks recorded before you switched it on can be sent with Send earlier tracks to Health, or one at a time from a track's details.

Northern Ireland

Ordnance Survey maps Great Britain: England, Scotland and Wales. Northern Ireland is mapped separately and isn't covered.

Contact

Questions and problems can be sent to silas.slack@gmail.com. It helps to include the iPhone model, the version of iOS, roughly where you were, and what you were doing when the problem happened.
