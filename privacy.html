Privacy

Updated 25 November 2026.

In short

Wanderer has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Your location, your recorded tracks and your planned routes stay on your iPhone. Nothing is collected about you, and nothing is sold or shared.

Your location

Wanderer uses your location to show where you are on the map and, while you're recording, to draw your track. Your position is used on the phone and is never sent anywhere.

What is stored

Recorded tracks and saved routes are kept on your iPhone, in Wanderer's folder in the Files app, where you can see, copy and delete them. Maps and footpaths you've viewed or downloaded are kept on the phone so they work offline. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What goes over the internet

To show maps and plan routes, Wanderer downloads them as you need them. These requests contain nothing about you, but like any request on the internet they come from your connection's IP address, and the pieces requested show which part of the country you're looking at:

  • Map pictures come from Ordnance Survey's map service (api.os.uk).
  • Footpath data comes from Wanderer's own copy of OpenStreetMap, served through Amazon CloudFront, in squares of 10 km. If that can't be reached, Wanderer asks OpenStreetMap's public Overpass service (overpass-api.de) instead.

Neither your location nor your tracks or routes are sent in these requests, and Wanderer keeps no record of them. Ordnance Survey, Amazon and the Overpass service may keep ordinary server logs under their own policies.

Permissions

  • Location, to show your position and record tracks, including with the phone locked while a recording is under way.
  • Motion & Fitness, to measure how much you climb with the barometer.

You can change these at any time in the iPhone Settings app.
